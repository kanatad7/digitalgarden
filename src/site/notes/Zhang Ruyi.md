Zhang Ruyi’s artistic practice occupies a unique space which reconciles artefacts, the industrial experience and urban life. The artist finds inspiration in everyday materials and explores the hierarchical interactions between individuals, materials and places. Her work mainly involves installation, sculpture and integrated media. She uses reality as a model to draw out moments of individual perception as the city shifts around its inhabitants, planting them within the material to bestow significance that extends beyond the material.
