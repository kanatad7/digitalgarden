Para Site藝術空間三十週年系列項目由「易過借房」揭開序幕。展覽以1996年機構創辦之初的同名展覽為參照，回應當年所探索的城市空間、記憶與藝術創作等議題，並立足今天的城市脈絡，反思這些問題如何隨時代演變。
Marking the beginning of a year-long series of programmes commemorating ParaSite's thirtieth anniversary, 'Site-seeing' takes its point of departure from an exhibition of the same name presented in 1996, the first year of the institution's operation.
While the original exhibition explored questions of urban space, memory and art-making, this iteration explores how these concerns have evolved as we navigate today's cities.

參展藝術家生於1970至1990年代，透過其委約創作及近期作品，共同回應當代城市生活中持續不斷又錯綜複雜的重建與改造。這些作品將香港置於與遠近城市的對話之中，探究驅動都市變遷的種種公共與私人利益。它們將城市呈現為一個在規管與疏離下形成的空間，同時亦捕捉在種種限制之間浮現驚奇、幽默與意義的時刻。作品探問記憶、欲望與焦慮，探討它們如何在變動不居的環境中流轉，又如何在建造、拆除與再造的循環中凝聚。
Through commissioned and recent works, 'Site-seeing' features a generation of artists born between the 1970s and 1990s. Their works collectively address the complexities of near-constant redevelopment that define much of contemporary urban life. By placing Hong Kong in dialogue with neighboring and distant cifies, the works probe into the myriad of public and private interests that drive urban change, staging the city as a space shaped by regulation, control and alienation. Yet, many of the works attend to the moments of wonder, humour, and meaning that persist within the cracks of the built environment. Across these shifting environments, memory, desire and anxiety circulate and gather through what is built, erased and remade.

三十年來，Para Site 與香港的城市肌理緊密相連，從堅尼地城遷到上環，及後再移至鰂魚涌。在區內都市化進程不斷加速的背景下，「易過借房」追湖城市空間如何成為我們生
活的中介，尋究我們觀看、感受與棲居的不同方式如何被協商與重構
Over three decades, Para Site has remained embedded in Hong Kong's urban fabric, moving from Kennedy Town to Sheung Wan and later to Quarry Bay.
Witnessing the accelerating pace of urbanisation across the region 'Site-seeing' traces how urban space mediates our lives, and how ways of seeing and inhabiting the city are negotiated across changing contexts.
