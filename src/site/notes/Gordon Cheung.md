Gordon Cheung is a contemporary multimedia artist and has developed an innovative approach to making art that blurs virtual and actual reality to reflect on the existential questions of what it means to be human in civilisations with histories written by the victors. Cheung raises questions about the effects of global capitalism and its underlying mechanisms of power on our perception of identity, territory and sense of belonging. These narratives are refracted through the prisms of culture, mythology, religion and politics into dreamlike spaces of surreal, urban worlds rooted in his in-between identity.
